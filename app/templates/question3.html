Visualization Guide

This heatmap visualization compares sentiment patterns across the three datasets (TROUT, FILAH, and complete journalist dataset). Each cell represents a person-topic combination:

  • X-axis: Topics (grouped by tourism vs. fishing)
  • Y-axis: People/Entities
  • Rectangles: Plans with sentiment-based coloring
  • Circles: Discussions with sentiment-based coloring
  • Hatched pattern: Missing data in incomplete datasets

Color Legend

Negative sentiment (-1.0)
Neutral sentiment (0.0)
Positive sentiment (+1.0)
Missing data (incomplete datasets)

Analysis Questions:

  • How do sentiment patterns differ between TROUT and FILAH datasets?
  • What additional insights emerge when viewing the complete journalist dataset?
  • Are TROUT's accusations strengthened, weakened, or unchanged in the complete context?
  • Which topics show the most significant differences between partial and complete datasets?
