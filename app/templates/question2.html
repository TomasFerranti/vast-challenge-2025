Analysis Summary

This visualization shows the distribution of sentiment scores for committee members (entity.person) across different industries. Each boxplot represents one industry's sentiment distribution, revealing potential biases in the COOTEFOO committee's attitudes.

How to Interpret:

  • Box: Contains 50% of the data (Q1 to Q3)

  • Sentiment scale: -1 (very negative) to +1 (very positive)

Key Insights:

  • Compare median sentiment across industries to identify bias
  • Look for industries with consistently positive or negative sentiment
  • Outliers may indicate specific controversial topics or strong opinions
  • Sample size (n=X) indicates how many sentiment entries each industry has
